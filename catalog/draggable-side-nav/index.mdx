`layout.DraggableSideNav` keeps its expanded width in a `*float32` and its state in a `*bool`. The tab on its edge, shown on hover, toggles it; a drag resizes it, and leaving it under a third of the way from `MinWidth` to its width collapses it. The body, `Header` and `Footer` get a `SideNavState`: `Expanded` says which content to build, kept at its width and clipped while the nav animates, and `Collapse` goes from 0 to 1 with the width. Given to `List`'s `Collapse` with `RailWidth`, it slides the rows into centered squares along the way.
