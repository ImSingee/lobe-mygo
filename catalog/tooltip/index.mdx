`overlay.Tooltip` attaches a tip to any `ui.Element`. It shows after the pointer rests for 0.6 seconds, at once when the keyboard focus arrives, and describes the element to assistive technology. It comes in on `motion.Tooltip`, from the side of its anchor.

## Basic Usage

`Hotkey` shows a key combination after the text.

<GoDemo name="tooltip/basic" title="Basic usage" height={160} source={basic} />

## Placement

`Top`, `Bottom`, `Left` and `Right` pick the side; where there is no room, the tip moves to the opposite side and its motion follows.
