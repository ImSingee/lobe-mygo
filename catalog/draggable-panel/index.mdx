`layout.DraggablePanel` keeps its size in a `*float32` and its state in a `*bool`. The seam lights up as the pointer nears it and turns primary while dragging; the button on it collapses the panel, whose content slides out at its own width rather than squeezing.

## Placement and Collapse

`Placement` picks the edge. With `CollapseThreshold`, dragging the panel that small collapses it, and it reopens at the size it had before the drag.
