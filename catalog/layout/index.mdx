`layout.Layout` holds the `Header` at the top. The `Sidebar` sits in a `DraggablePanel` and scrolls on its own; the content, the `Toc` and the `Footer` scroll under the header, the table of contents held at the top. `Mobile` leaves out the sidebar and moves the table of contents into the header.
