`display.Avatar` draws a name as marbled colors by default: the same name always gets the same colors, in light and dark.

## Basic Usage

<GoDemo name="avatar/basic" title="Marble" height={120} source={basic} />

## Text and Emoji

`AvatarText` shows the first two letters on the border color, or on `Background` in the color that reads on it. An emoji shows as is.

<GoDemo name="avatar/fallback" title="Text and emoji" height={120} source={fallback} />

## Sizes and Shapes

Square corners round by a sixth of the size; `AvatarCircle` makes it round.
