`layout.ScrollArea` scrolls the content you pass it. Its thumbs sit 3 DIPs in from the edges, 3 DIPs thin and 6 under the pointer; they show at once while the content scrolls, stay half a second after, fade in while the pointer is over the area, and scroll the content as they are dragged. `Fade` fades the content over 40 DIPs toward each edge it can still scroll toward, and drops the fade at an edge it has reached.
