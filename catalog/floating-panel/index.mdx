`feedback.FloatingPanel` shows while its `*bool` is true, `Offset` DIPs in from the `Placement` corner, 640 DIPs wide unless `Width` says otherwise and never more than the window less 32. The page behind it stays usable: a press on it, Escape or the close button closes the panel, unless `NoOutsideClose` or `NoKeyboard` say otherwise. The edges facing away from its corner resize it between `MinWidth`/`MinHeight` and `MaxWidth`/`MaxHeight`.
