`feedback.FloatingSheet` sits along the bottom of its container while its `*bool` is true. Heights up to 1 are fractions of the container's height. Dragging the header resizes it between `MinHeight` and `MaxHeight`, or to the closest of its `SnapPoints`, where a quick flick goes to the next one; dragging it under `CloseThreshold` of the lowest closes it, unless `NoDismiss`.

## Inline

`Inline` puts the sheet in the container's flow, which keeps the room it rests at; dragged taller, it covers the content above. `Embedded` draws a border instead of a shadow.
