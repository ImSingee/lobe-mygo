`field.DatePicker` opens a `field.Calendar` below its trigger and closes once a day is chosen. The month and year in the header open views to choose them; the arrows, Page Up and Page Down move between days while a day has the focus, and Enter chooses one.

`DisabledDate` keeps days from being chosen; `WeekStart` starts the weeks on another day.
