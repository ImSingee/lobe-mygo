`feedback.Modal` shows content in a dialog while a `*bool` is true, and keeps the focus inside it. Cancel, the close button, Escape and a click on the backdrop close it. OK returns `true` and leaves `*open` alone, so the caller closes it once its action succeeds. The panel scales in on `motion.Modal`.
