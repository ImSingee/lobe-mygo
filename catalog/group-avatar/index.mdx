`display.GroupAvatar` lays out up to four members in two columns, or nine in three, on a light fill; one member shows large. `GroupCornerCircle` shrinks the avatars to fit a circle. Lobe draws the squircle corners with an SVG mask; mygo has no mask of a path, so they are rounded squares nearest each curve.
