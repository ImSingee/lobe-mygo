`feedback.Alert` shows a message with the icon and color of its `Type`. The soft surface mixes 5% of that color into the container, with a 14% ring.

## Description and Close

A `Description` makes the alert roomier and its icon larger. `Closed` adds a close button that sets it, and the alert collapses away.
